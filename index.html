research

My research spans three areas:

deep learning
Design, training, and evaluation of neural network architectures, including multimodal models that integrate imaging and tabular data.
e.g. convolutional networks, attention-based fusion, PyTorch
image processing and computer vision
Methods for detection, segmentation, classification, and quantitative feature extraction from images.
e.g. medical imaging (mammography, X-ray), microscopy, embedded vision for autonomous vehicles
applied machine learning
Development and deployment of machine learning systems, including data pipelines, experiment tracking, and cloud infrastructure.
e.g. credit risk modeling, data quality, MLflow, AWS, Docker

My doctoral research focuses on medical image analysis for breast cancer, with an emphasis on mammography. A list of publications is available on Google Scholar.
